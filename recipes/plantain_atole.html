Plantain Atole (Atol de plátano)

To make Plantain Atole mature bananas are used. You can find this delicious beverage on patronal fair posts and informal sales, perfect for a cold day with a delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 or 3 mature plantains
  • 6 cups of water
  • Cinnamon stick
  • ½ cup of sugar

Steps

  1. Put water in a pot to heat.
  2. Cut the plantains into large pieces with peel and tips also cut.
  3. When the water began to boil, put the plantains with peel.
  4. Cook the plantains until they get soft.
  5. When they are ready, take them out of the water, peels them and put them to blend with a little bit of water (the same that were cooked).
  6. Add the blended plantains to the water where they were cooked before.
  7. Add sugar and cinnamon to taste.
  8. Heat over mid-low heat, stirring it and being careful when starts to boil, not going to overflow.
  9. Enjoy!
