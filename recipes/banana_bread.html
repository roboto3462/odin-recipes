Banana Bread

This will show how to make this classic delicious guatemalan banana bread.

Ingredients

  • 2 butter sticks
  • 2 cups of sugar
  • 3 cups of flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup of cream
  • 2 cups of mashed banana
  • 2 teaspoons of vanilla
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 tablespoons of baking soda
  • 3 sliced bananas

Steps

  1. Sift the flour together with the salt and place it in a bowl.
  2. Beat the butter until it has a creamy texture, then mix with the sugar, eggs and vanilla and continuing to beat until you get a homogeneous mixture.
  3. Then incorporate the cream and the baking soda into the mixture, then add the mashed banana.
  4. After that, add the flour and beat until the mixture is fully combined.
  5. Grease and flour a mold, the pour in the mixture, adding some banana slices in the center.
  6. Afterwards, place it in the oven and bake at 120ºC (248ºF) for 80 minutes.
  7. Enjoy!
