Guatemalan Rellenitos

This will show how to make delicious Guatemalan Rellenitos, made for everyone, made with plantain and beans.

Ingredients

  • 4 mature plantains
  • 1 can of refried black beans
  • Sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • A cinnamon stick
  • Cinnamon powder

Steps

  1. Clean, cut the plantains and put them on a pot with water to boil them, add a cinnamon stick and boil them for 15 to 20 minutes until they get soft.
  2. Take them out of the water and peel them, beat them to get a plantain dough, if it's very thick, add a bit of water you used before and if it's too liquid you can add a but of flour.
  3. Place the beans in a pan over low heat and season with sugar and cinnamon power to taste.
  4. With wet hands, take the plantain dough and form a cake that's neither too large nor too thin. Place the beans in the middle and fold them together, making sure they're not visible.
  5. Place a pan with enough oil. Once the oil is hot, carefully place all the rellenitos in the pan to fry them. Once the rellenitos are fried, place them on paper towels to absorb the oil. They're ready to serve.
  6. Enjoy!
